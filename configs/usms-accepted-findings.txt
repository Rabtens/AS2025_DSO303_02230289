usms-ec2-app-role | trust policy: service principal ec2.amazonaws.com | usms-admin-01 | 2027-05-10 | EC2 does not send aws:SourceArn/aws:SourceAccount when it assumes an instance-profile role, so a condition would block the instance. Mitigated: no user, group or role is granted iam:PassRole on it (the only PassRole grant is usms-deploy-role -> three ECS roles, ecs-tasks only).
